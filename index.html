Coupled Non-Linear Electromagnetic Repulsion & Micro-Truss Yield Dynamics

Active magnetic deflection of relativistic Galactic Cosmic Rays ($Z \ge 1$, $E_k \sim 1 \text{ to } 10 \text{ GeV/nucleon}$) requires establishing a massive magnetic moment ($M \sim 10^7 \text{ A}\cdot\text{m}^2$) using lightweight, deployable high-temperature superconducting (HTS) coils. The fundamental engineering wall is the Biot-Savart mutual Lorentz force ($F = I \times B$): the same field that protects astronauts places hundreds of kilonewtons of bursting and buckling stress on ultralight titanium/composite deployable booms.

01 Relativistic Ion Deflection & Mutual Lorentz Stress

Lorentz Mechanics

Relativistic cosmic ions of charge $q = Z e$ and velocity $\mathbf{v}$ obey the Lorentz trajectory equation inside the non-uniform magnetic dipole field $\mathbf{B}(\mathbf{r})$ generated by the multi-coil superconducting shield:

$$\frac{d}{dt}\left( \gamma m_0 \mathbf{v} \right) = q \left( \mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}(\mathbf{r}) \right), \quad \gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}}$$

Simultaneously, each superconducting coil segment carrying current $I_i$ experiences an outward bursting stress and mutual repulsion against adjacent coils $j$ given by the Biot-Savart integral over the deployable boom perimeter:

$$\mathbf{F}_{ij} = \frac{\mu_0 I_i I_j}{4\pi} \oint_{C_i} \oint_{C_j} \frac{d\boldsymbol{\ell}_i \times (d\boldsymbol{\ell}_j \times \hat{\mathbf{r}}_{ji})}{|\mathbf{r}_{ji}|^2}$$

For deep space GCR deflection shields exceeding $5 \text{ T}\cdot\text{m}$ bending power, mutual inter-coil repulsion forces exceed $F \ge 85 \text{ kN}$, creating critical compressive Euler buckling loads on connecting deployable struts.

02 Elastic-Plastic Truss Transition & Hysteresis

Constitutive Mechanics

Ultralight deployable trusses utilize high-modulus carbon-fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) tubes bonded to Ti-6Al-4V nodal fittings. Under nominal cyclic magnetic ramping, the structural response is purely elastic:

$$\sigma(t) = E_0 \, \varepsilon(t), \quad \Phi(t) = L_0 \, I(t) + \sum_{k} M_{ik} \, I_k(t)$$

When electromagnetic repulsion approaches the critical Euler buckling limit $P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2 E I}{(\kappa L)^2}$, micro-buckling and resin microcracking trigger non-linear plastic displacement ($\delta_p$). The mutual inductance matrix becomes non-linear:

$$\oint (F \, d\Phi - \Phi \, dF) = \mathcal{H}_{\text{hysteresis}} = \oint \sigma(\varepsilon) \, d\varepsilon > 0$$

A non-zero enclosed contour area $\mathcal{H}_{\text{hysteresis}}$ in $(F, \Phi)$ space proves irreversible structural deformation. If uncorrected, this hysteresis leads immediately to catastrophic truss snap-through and coil thermal quench.

Real-Time Inversion

$$\Delta \lambda_B = \lambda_B \left( 1 - p_e \right) \varepsilon_{\text{truss}} + \xi \, \Delta T_{\text{cryo}}$$

$$\Psi_{\text{sentinel}}(t) = \int_{t - \tau}^{t} \left[ F(\tau') \frac{d\Phi}{d\tau'} - \Phi(\tau') \frac{dF}{d\tau'} \right] d\tau'$$
